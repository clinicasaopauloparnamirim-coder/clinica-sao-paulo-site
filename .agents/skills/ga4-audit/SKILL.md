# ga4-audit

## Procedure
1. Identify the GA4 property and timezone/currency.
2. Verify Google Ads link.
3. Inspect event volume and key events.
4. Verify critical events such as clique_whatsapp and generate_lead.
5. Compare Ads conversion actions with GA4 event availability.
6. Flag duplicate or clearly misplaced key events.
7. Never delete or modify GA4 settings during an audit.
## Output
State the exact property, date window, event evidence and any measurement gap.
