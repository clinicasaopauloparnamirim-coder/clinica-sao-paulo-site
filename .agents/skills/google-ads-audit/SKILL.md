# google-ads-audit

## Procedure
1. Identify the customer account and campaign.
2. Read campaign status, serving status, channel, budget, bid strategy and network settings.
3. Read locations, languages, ad groups, ads, keywords, negatives, search terms, landing pages, assets and policy status.
4. Read conversion actions and identify primary versus observation actions.
5. Compare every final URL to the current website content.
6. Separate observed facts from recommendations.
7. Prepare launch artifacts but do not mutate the account.
## Safety
- No budget changes.
- No campaign activation.
- No payment.
- No mutation unless explicitly confirmed by the user.
